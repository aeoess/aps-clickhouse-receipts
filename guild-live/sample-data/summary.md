During Q1, the receipt pipeline transitioned from batch to streaming inserts, which successfully reduced median insert latency from 900ms to 140ms. Although two incidents occurred due to schema drift in the traces table, the system became more efficient. In Q2, verification shifted to read-time re-checks, leading to the discovery of a misconfigured scope on the deploy agent. Additionally, dashboard adoption grew significantly, and the audit view has now become the default landing page.
